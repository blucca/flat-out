# FLAT OUT. — RenderJuice Speed submission

The clean 16-second film is complete. This pack is ready for Blucca's normal
account registration and contest submission.

## Enter

1. Create or sign in to a free [RenderJuice account](https://www.app.renderjuice.com/).
2. Join the [contest Discord](https://discord.gg/FpAEQPpKvv).
3. In `#start-here`, select **Join contest** and complete the private form.
   Use the account holder's own email and details. Read the
   [Speed brief](https://www.renderjuice.com/contests/speed) and
   [contest terms](https://www.renderjuice.com/contests/terms) when entering.
4. In `#contest-submissions`, post the text below and attach the
   [Blender work-in-progress image](https://blucca.github.io/flat-out/stills/blender-wip.png).
   The public film link is supported; the [MP4](https://blucca.github.io/flat-out/film.mp4)
   is also available for direct upload.
5. Save the Discord message link as the submission receipt.

**Deadline:** October 31, 2026, 23:59 UTC / November 1, 07:59 China Standard Time.
Registration and posting are completed personally by the account holder,
as requested by the organizer. The existing film was rendered locally.

FLAT OUT.
